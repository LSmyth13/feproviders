For employers

Find an FE college near you

Further Education colleges are publicly funded institutions that work directly with local employers. They can train your existing workforce, deliver apprenticeships on your behalf, provide work placement students at no cost, and design bespoke programmes around your specific skills gaps — often with government funding available to help cover costs.

Use the map to find colleges in your area. Click any pin to see contact details, then reach out directly — most colleges have a dedicated employer or business team who will be happy to discuss your needs.

What FE providers can offer employers
Colleges offer far more than full-time courses — bespoke training and employer partnerships are commonplace
🤝
Bespoke Training
Many colleges design and deliver training tailored to your workforce — from half-day sessions to multi-year programmes.
📋
Apprenticeship Delivery
FE colleges are England's largest apprenticeship delivery route, covering technical, professional and management standards.
🏭
Industry Placements
T Level students complete 315+ hours of industry placement — a direct pipeline to emerging talent.
📊
Skills Bootcamps
Government-funded intensive training (16–19 weeks) across digital, green skills, construction and healthcare — free for your staff.
🏆
Professional Qualifications
NVQs, BTECs, City & Guilds and sector-specific licences to practise — all nationally recognised.
💻
Short Courses & CPD
From first aid to data analysis — flexible short courses delivered on your site or at the college.
🌱
Employer Engagement
Colleges seek employer advisory board members, guest speakers and mentors to shape curriculum — great for brand visibility.
🔬
Specialist Facilities
Industry-standard workshops, labs, studios and digital suites available for hire, with expert staff on hand.
